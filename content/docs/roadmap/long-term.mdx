The long-term goal is a standard runtime layer that proves live human presence and action-bound consent across voice-AI and autonomous agent channels without biometric identity vaults.

## Human-presence and consent firewall

Applications should verify whether a live human authorized a privileged tool call with the same predictability as verifying a TLS certificate or OIDC token. Downstream APIs and MCP servers verify signed permits offline in milliseconds without building custom anti-spoofing pipelines.

## Adversarial adaptation

Neural speech synthesis and live voice conversion improve every quarter. Detection models and calibrated operating points must evolve against sealed, speaker-isolated, vocoder-disjoint blind evaluations rather than static benchmark sets.

## What stays constant

- **Zero-retention privacy boundary.** Entros builds no face, iris, palm, or voiceprint registries and requires no government ID. Entros scores voice audio in memory with zero disk persistence.
- **Empirical truth.** Every published detection number cites a reproducible held-out evaluation with exact denominators, model version, and codec profile. Models that miss preregistered gates do not ship to production.
- **Permissive commercial licensing.** Production models and consumer verification SDKs stay under permissive commercial licenses (`MIT`, `Apache-2.0`, `BSD-3-Clause`) with zero GPL dependencies in `@entros/verify`.
- **Token decoupling.** `$ENTROS` stays separate from Entros security products, API billing, and engineering roadmaps.
