Medium-term work expands the firewall from developer voice-AI and MCP deployments into regulated contact centers and enterprise compliance pipelines.

## Narrowband telephony and VPC expansion

Once narrowband `8 kHz` telephony blind gates pass, Entros will package zero-voiceprint VPC sidecars for PSTN and SIP contact centers and IT help desks. Deployment will require no GPU cluster and store no caller voiceprint registry.

## Compliance receipts and decomposed policy

Planned `Policy v2` contracts keep evidence freshness, assurance tier, modality, continuity, uniqueness status, and canonical action payload hashes as separate fields. Lower assurance tiers will not satisfy higher policy rules.

Each authorization decision will emit a verifiable `IETF SCITT` transparency receipt for regulated audit logs.

## Optional on-chain audit anchoring

Enterprise buyers that want third-party chain non-repudiation can opt into batch Merkle-root anchoring of compliance receipts. On-chain anchoring stays off by default and requires zero crypto wallets in standard Web2 deployments.

Resuming the paused Solana trusted-setup ceremony, independent security audit, and mainnet deployment gates depends on concrete customer demand for on-chain anchoring.
