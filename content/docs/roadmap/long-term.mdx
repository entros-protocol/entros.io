The long-term aim is a Solana-native human-control signal that any program can read. The protocol is designed to become public infrastructure.

## Identity as public infrastructure

The mission is to make a fresh human-control check as composable as a wallet signature. Integrators should read one documented state format across applications.

The planned economics route fees to validators and a governed treasury after the validator network exists. Current fees accrue to the protocol treasury.

## Cross-chain as a default

A wallet's current verification state is a Solana account. Future relay work can make that state readable on other chains. The relay design must state its bridge and finality assumptions.

## Behavioral as a moving target

Voice, motion, and touch synthesis will improve. The validation policy must evolve against new measured attacks. A future validator network needs a controlled update and attestation process for that policy.

## What stays constant

- **Privacy boundary.** Raw motion and full-resolution touch stay on the client. Phrase audio leaves for transient processing and must never persist.
- **Bounded mission.** Entros is designed to answer whether a wallet presents current and returning human-control evidence. It does not verify legal identity or personal attributes.
- **Public protocol.** The on-chain programs, client SDK, circuits, and research paper remain public. The private validation engine publishes scoped campaign results and safe resolved-finding summaries.
